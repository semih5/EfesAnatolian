Our Kitchen

Authentic flavors, warm hospitality

Efes Anatolian Kitchen brings the recipes of Turkey to Bucks County. From the first cup of Turkish tea to the last bite of baklava, everything is made to be shared.

Charcoal Grilled

Adana kebab, lamb chops, skewers and mixed grill, cooked over open charcoal the traditional way.

Fresh From the Oven

Turkish pide, lahmacun and flatbreads baked to order, plus fresh Turkish loaf bread with breakfast.

100% Halal & BYOB

All of our meat is halal. Bring your own bottle to enjoy with dinner; we are happy to serve you.
